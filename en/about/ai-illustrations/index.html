Methods & evidence · AI images

AI-generated illustrations

When no usable historical image exists, or no free/commercial licence can be cleared, Shilu uses AI-generated explanatory illustrations. They are not historical photographs and not evidence.

Labels and limits

  • The type is always illustration and the page shows an “AI-generated illustration” badge;
  • Never presented as a historical photo, artefact photo, portrait or document; no identifiable real faces;
  • Captions describe what is depicted and remain bound to the claim ledger; no readable text, fake inscriptions or modern marks;
  • Every image stays pending-human-review until a human checks it;
  • The image explains; the textual source and claim ledger remain the factual basis.
