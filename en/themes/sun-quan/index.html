Sun Quan: A Successor's Vassalage and Imperial Claim

Theme

The Sun Quan theme gathers the person-centered story built around Sun Quan himself, from Sun Ce's pacification of Jiangdong in 195 to Sun Quan's death in Jianye in 252. It first sets out the foundation left by Sun Ce, then follows Sun Quan succeeding and stabilizing the situation, moving the capital to Jianye and building Stone City. Facing Liu Bei's invasion, he declared vassalage to Cao Pi and accepted the King of Wu title, yet refused a hostage and built his own bureaucracy, before proclaiming himself emperor at Wuchang, sending a fleet to Yizhou, and leaving a young son to succeed him. Unlike the Red Cliffs and Xiaoting campaign stories, this line does not expand on battle details; it follows how this successor weighed alliance and vassalage to secure Jiangdong and gradually earn imperial legitimacy. Read it alongside the event stories and the Liu Bei and Zhuge Liang stories; core figures with archives are listed in the person files. This theme note is navigation only, not a biography.
