Methods & evidence

Page

Structural volume map vs curated stories

The frozen story map sorts every event into a structural unit with a chapter position. A structural unit is an editorial slot in the volume; it has no narrative text by itself. A curated story is a separately published reading path with written zh/en chapters, a period, a depth tier and an evidence status. Being mapped to a structural unit does not mean a story page exists.

What legacyNarrative is

Every event keeps a legacy narrative copied from the 2026-11-22 legacy book (`legacy-book-2026-11-22`). It is an auto-organized digest of the old entry, shown under the legacy-narrative block for reading continuity only. It is not a curated story chapter, and legacy AI-generated text is not evidence. Where an event has a refined override, the refined text is presented separately from this legacy digest.

Depth tiers and evidence statuses

Depth tiers describe editorial depth, not source completeness: L1 is a single-axis pilot read, L2 compares across sources, L3 is deep synthesis. Evidence statuses are separate: `sourced` means the chapter events carry source-linked cards; `partial` means some but not all events are source-linked; `legacy` means the read still rests on legacy material pending sources. Verification level on an event is a separate record and does not promote a legacy story to sourced.

AI-generated images and labeling

AI-assisted or generated imagery is labeled where it appears. It illustrates a mood or period setting and is not evidence for a fact, portrait, battle scene or material culture. Source-linked claims remain tied to their cited records; images never upgrade a verification level.

Why structural units have no story pages yet

The frozen map assigns events to 1,128 structural units, but writing a curated story requires checked chapters, bilingual translation and a verified cast. Until that work finishes, these units keep their map position and show only the volume note; the index never lists them as published stories.

How refined events are handled

Eight event-level overrides were refined on 2026-11-22. They carry a refined badge on their event page, keep their legacy digest in a separate block, and have not been turned into standalone story pages. Promoting a refined event to a story page remains a later editorial decision.

Dates

Historical years have no year zero. Approximate, contested and interval dates must be distinguished from exact dates. Calendar precision is retained rather than invented.

Evidence

Imported, pending review, source-checked and disputed are separate editorial states. Source-checked entries state their review scope; this does not certify unreviewed coordinates or other entries. Structural checks and length scores cannot verify historical facts.

Geography and civilizations

The basemap is geographical. Contemporary country outlines are not presented as historical political borders. Civilization bands describe content coverage and editorial phases.

Translations

Languages share stable event IDs and fact fields. Missing or outdated translations remain visibly marked.
