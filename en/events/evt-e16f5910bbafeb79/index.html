Original entry and sentence-level review

The Rus' Primary Chronicle begins its dated narrative at 8594, recording that Varangians from overseas took tribute from the Chud, Slavs, Merya, Ves and Krivichi, while the Khazars exacted 'a white squirrel skin per household' from the Polianians, Severians and Viatichi.1 Though set down long afterwards, the entry sketches the dual overlordship of the mid-ninth-century East European forest belt: Scandinavian merchant-warriors in the north, the Khazar Khaganate in the south.2 Two years later the tribes expelled the Varangians and fell to feuding, leading to the invitation of Rurik in 862 and the legendary beginning of the Rus' state.3
