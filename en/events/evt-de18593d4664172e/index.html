Chapter 3 of 8
Previous chapterEnvoy to Jiangdong, securing the Sun–Liu allianceNext chapterSends Deng Zhi to repair the Shu–Wu alliance

After Liu Bei's death, his son Liu Shan ascended the throne, changing the era name to Jianxing. The event record says Zhuge Liang, as Chancellor and Minister over the Masses, opened his office and effectively held all power.

Once in charge, Zhuge Liang set about rebuilding relations with Eastern Wu, sending Deng Zhi as envoy to Wu. Seeing that Zhuge Liang was in charge, Sun Quan reciprocated by sending Zhang Wen to Shu, and the two sides agreed to attack Wei together. The record states the Shu–Wu alliance was restored, laying the foundation for later northern expeditions and Wei–Wu wars.

In the person-centered line, this chapter turns Zhuge Liang from strategist into head of government: under a young ruler, the chancellor's office took charge of Shu affairs. The record does not detail the staff of the office or the internal reforms that followed.
