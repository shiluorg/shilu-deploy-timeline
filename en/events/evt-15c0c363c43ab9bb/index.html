Friedrich Engels is born

Event
1820 CEGermanyThoughtLegacy import · pending source reviewL1 · Structure checked · AI expansion

Volume position: Frankish & Medieval Europe · Ch. 4/8

Locate on the globe ↗Compare civilizations ↗More in this year ↗

Engels and Marx co-founded historical materialism and wrote "Anti-Dühring" and other works, which profoundly shaped modern socialist thought.1 In his early years, he observed the conditions of British industrial cities and wrote about the living and working conditions of the working class.2 After co-writing the "Communist Manifesto" with Marx, he played a long-term role in theoretical and political organizations.3 "Anti-Dühring" systematically elaborated on the relationship between philosophy, political economics and socialism, and became one of the systematic works of Marxism.4
