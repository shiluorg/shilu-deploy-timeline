Original entry and sentence-level review

In 720, during the reign of Empress Gensho of Japan, the court ordered the compilation of the Nihon Shoki.1 This work, in 30 volumes and written in classical Chinese, is the oldest official history of Japan.2 It covers from the mythological age to the reign of Empress Jito, blending legend and fact to establish the divine origin of the emperor.3 Empress Gensho was the fifth female emperor of Japan and continued to promote the ritsuryō system and state protection of Buddhism.4
