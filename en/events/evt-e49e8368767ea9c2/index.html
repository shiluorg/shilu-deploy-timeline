This translation predates the current source revision and needs review. Chinese source ↗

Shalmaneser III marched to the eastern Mediterranean coast.1,2 The Phoenician city-states of Tyre, Sidon, and Byblos submitted and paid tribute to avoid war. Assyrian forces did not enter the city-states but occupied the surrounding farmland and controlled port revenues. The king of Tyre offered 120 talents (about 3.6 tons) of silver, along with cedar wood, ivory, and purple cloth. Assyria confirmed the city-states' internal autonomy in writing, establishing a vassal relationship between Assyria and Phoenicia.1
