Using the 'Rites of Zhou' as a blueprint, Wang Mang established strict hierarchical regulations for carriages and clothing, specifying styles and colors according to official rank.1 He also standardized marriage rituals and unified standards for betrothal gifts and dowries. Aimed at restoring social order through ancient rites, this laid the groundwork for his 'reforms by entrusting antiquity'.1
