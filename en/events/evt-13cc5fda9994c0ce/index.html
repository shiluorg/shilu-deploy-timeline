Height of the Maya Kingdom of Copán

In the mid-8th century, Maya civilization entered its Late Classic period, and the kingdom of Copán reached its peak.1 Copán, located in modern western Honduras, was one of the most important Maya city-states.2 King Uaxaclajuun Ubʼaah Kʼawiil (r. 695–738) built numerous pyramids, altars, and stelae. Copán's Hieroglyphic Stairway is the longest carved Maya inscription. In 738, the king was captured and executed by the rival city-state of Quiriguá, and Copán entered a period of decline.
