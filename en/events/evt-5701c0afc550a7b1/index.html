Li Su's snow-night attack on Caizhou captures Wu Yuanji

This translation predates the current source revision and needs review. Chinese source ↗

On 15 October 817, under heavy snow, Li Su led 9,000 elite troops on a 130-li forced march straight to the Huaixi headquarters at Caizhou.1 The government forces broke through the outer and inner walls to the city, capturing Wu Yuanji in his sleep.2 He was sent to Chang'an and executed.3 The three prefectures of Huaixi were divided into three circuits, with court-appointed jiedushi.4 Li Su's snow-night attack became a classic example of surprise attack in ancient military history.5
