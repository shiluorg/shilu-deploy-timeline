Cree subarctic hunting society

Event
1600 CESubarctic CanadaSocietyLegacy import · pending source reviewL1 · Structure checked · AI expansion

Volume position: Global Civilizations · Ch. 7/8

Locate on the globe ↗Compare civilizations ↗More in this year ↗

The Cree people are widely distributed in the subarctic forests and tundra of Canada.1 They make a living by hunting moose and bison and trading furs.2 They are one of the most widely distributed indigenous peoples in North America.3 They migrated seasonally between forests and tundra, organized hunts in families and local groups, and the fur trade connected inland hunting grounds to trading posts on the Hudson Bay.4 The lifestyle is adapted to the cold and sparse resources of the northern environment.5
