Cao Cao kills Lü Bu

In 198, Cao Cao besieged Xiapi, in the area of present-day Suining, Jiangsu. The event record says he captured and executed Lü Bu and his generals Chen Gong and Gao Shun.

The record describes Lü Bu as treacherous and ultimately self-destroyed. Cao Cao accepted the surrender of Zhang Liao and others, and Xuzhou was incorporated into his domains. This chapter removed Lü Bu's force from the eastern front, leaving no direct separatist ruler to contain Cao Cao in the Yan and Xuzhou direction.

The record does not detail the later appointments of Lü Bu's surrendered generals. In the person-centered line, eliminating Lü Bu cleared Cao Cao's eastern front before Guandu, leaving him room to concentrate against Yuan Shao in Hebei.
