Completion of Hwaseong Fortress

Event
1796 CEJoseonArchitectureLegacy import · pending source reviewL1 · Structure checked · AI expansion

Volume position: The Korean Peninsula · Ch. 2/6

Locate on the globe ↗Compare civilizations ↗More in this year ↗

King Jeongjo built a new city in order to relocate the royal crown prince's mausoleum.1 Jeong Yak-yong designed lifting machines and pulleys to save more than half of the labor.2 The city walls incorporated the advantages of east and west city construction.3 The engineering archive "Hwaseong Fortress Service Ritual" is exhaustive in detail.4 The city walls were made of bricks and stones, and the enemy towers and city gates were matched, taking into account both defense and ceremony.5 The machinery and construction methods were recorded in diagrams, which became the basis for later generations to study Korean city building.6 Engineering combines filial piety, technology and system in one place.7
