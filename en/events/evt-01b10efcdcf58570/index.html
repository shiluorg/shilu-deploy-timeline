In 229, urged on by his ministers, Sun Quan formally ascended the imperial throne at Wuchang, modern Ezhou, Hubei, proclaiming the dynasty name Wu and changing the era name to Huanglong. The event record says he posthumously honored his father Sun Jian as Emperor Wulie of Wu and his elder brother Sun Ce as Prince Huan of Changsha; he appointed Lu Xun Senior General-in-Chief and Zhuge Jin General-in-Chief.

After Sun Quan proclaimed himself emperor, Shu Han sent Chen Zhen to congratulate him. The two sides agreed that after destroying Wei they would divide the empire equally: the territory west of Hangu Pass to Shu, and east of the pass to Wu.

In the person-centered line, this chapter completes Sun Quan's legitimacy: from King of Wu under Wei to self-proclaimed emperor, the Jiangdong regime now held its full title. The record does not detail the enfeoffments after accession or the precise terms of the Shu alliance.
