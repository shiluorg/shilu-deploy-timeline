Battle of Yorktown: American victory in Revolutionary War

American General Washington and French General Rochambeau command 17,000 Franco-American forces (8,000 Americans, 9,000 French) and, with the help of French Admiral de Grasse, surround 8,000 British troops under General Cornwallis at Yorktown, Virginia. On October 17, Cornwallis, deeming his situation 'desperate', surrenders. The British band plays 'The World Turned Upside Down'. After the battle, Britain ceases major military operations, and the Treaty of Paris in 1783 recognizes American independence.

Yorktown was a joint land-sea operation: the French fleet closed the sea route while American and French troops surrounded Cornwallis. Without the French navy, Yorktown would not have ended in surrender.
