Liu Bei proclaims himself King of Hanzhong at Mianyang and establishes the Hanzhong King's office

In 219, urged on by his ministers, Liu Bei erected an altar at Mianyang (present-day Mian County, Shaanxi) and declared himself King of Hanzhong, continuing the Han imperial line as a member of the Han clan.

He made Xu Jing Grand Tutor, Fa Zheng Minister of the Interior, Guan Yu General of the Front, Zhang Fei General of the Right, Ma Chao General of the Left, and Huang Zhong General of the Rear. Liu Bei's authority in Shu was further consolidated, and his military and economic strength stood in a tripartite balance with Cao Wei and Eastern Wu.

The kingship marks the formation of Liu Bei's regime: holding Yizhou and Hanzhong, he was no longer merely a regional warlord but set up a royal office and appointed officials under a princely title. This arrangement also laid the institutional groundwork for his later imperial founding.
