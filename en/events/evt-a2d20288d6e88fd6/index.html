Around 2700 BCE, the Norte Chico center of Caral began building large stepped pyramid platforms (the largest about 18 m high) in the arid valleys of Peru. Caral people based their society on cotton-fish exchange and developed a peaceful trade society with no fortifications. Caral is one of the oldest urban sites in the Americas, dating back to 3000 BCE.

Caral’s pyramid platforms were not royal tombs but centres of public ritual and labour organisation. They show that a city could arise without writing or metal, on human labour and fishing surplus.
