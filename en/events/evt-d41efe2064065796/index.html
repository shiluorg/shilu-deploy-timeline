Sun Quan sends Zhuge Jin to negotiate with Liu Bei; dispute over Jingzhou temporarily eased

After Red Cliffs, Liu Bei had borrowed the Nan commandery, but Sun Quan believed Jingzhou belonged to Eastern Wu. The event record says Sun Quan sent Zhuge Jin, Zhuge Liang's elder brother, to Chengdu to demand the return of Changsha, Guiyang, and Lingling commanderies.

Liu Bei refused, saying he would return Jingzhou after taking Liangzhou. Sun Quan was furious and sent Lü Meng to seize the three commanderies, while Liu Bei sent Guan Yu to contest them. Then, because Cao Cao attacked Hanzhong and Liu Bei worried about the safety of Yizhou, he negotiated a truce with Sun Quan. They divided Jingzhou along the Xiang River: Changsha, Jiangxia, and Guiyang to Sun Quan; Nan, Lingling, and Wuling to Liu Bei.

This chapter shows the rift within the alliance was not truly healed. The record presents a surface settlement by boundary: the dispute over western Jingzhou remained, leaving a source for later conflict.
