Cao Cao and Liu Bei fight fiercely in Hanzhong; both sides reach a stalemate

Hanzhong: How the Triangle Solidified into Tripartite Rule

Next chapterMount Dingjun and the Han River

After Liu Bei was secure in Yizhou, Fa Zheng advised him to seize Hanzhong. Liu Bei personally led his army to attack Hanzhong, first sending Zhang Fei, Ma Chao, and Wu Lan against Xiabian (present-day Cheng County, Gansu), but this detachment was defeated by Cao Hong.

Liu Bei's main force stationed at Yangping Pass, facing Xiahou Yuan and Zhang He. Cao Cao then led a large army to reinforce, and the two sides were locked in stalemate for months, each winning and losing some encounters. Because grain transport was difficult, Cao Cao finally withdrew in 219, leaving Cao Hong and Cao Zhen to defend the area.

This chapter opens the Hanzhong standoff. Hanzhong was the gateway to Yizhou, and whoever held it controlled the initiative northward and eastward. Neither side could swallow the other in the opening phase, and the contest settled into a long stalemate.
