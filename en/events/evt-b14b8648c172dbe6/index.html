The Alamanni, Franks, and other Germanic tribes massed along the Rhine, and in some places Roman border defenses had become ineffective.1 The Germans crossed the Rhine, plundering villages in eastern Gaul, and the Roman army was forced to contract its defense lines.2 These invasions were a harbinger of the collapse of the empire's western frontier during the Crisis of the Third Century.3
