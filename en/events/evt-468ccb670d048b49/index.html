Original entry and sentence-level review

In 1796 the English physician Edward Jenner inoculated James Phipps, an eight-year-old boy, with cowpox.1 He then exposed Phipps to smallpox, and Phipps did not contract the disease.2 Jenner repeated the experiment on other children, including his own son.3 His findings were published in 1798.4 Placing “Jenner conducts a cowpox inoculation experiment” in 1796 Europe, it connects earlier and later change in Technology.5

In 1796, British doctor Edward Jenner vaccinated eight-year-old James Phipps with cowpox.6 He then exposed Phipps to smallpox, and Phipps did not become ill.6 Jenner repeated the experiment on other children, including his own son. The results were published in 1798. Putting "Jenner's cowpox vaccination experiment" back to England in 1796, it connects the changes in technology over a long period of time.7 This entry makes no claim to the first artificial immunisation in world history.
