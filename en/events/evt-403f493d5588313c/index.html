Mali Empire established: Mansa Musa's golden age

Gold Empires of West Africa: Ghana, Mali and Songhai
Chapter 3 of 7
Previous chapterThe Ghana Empire controls West African gold tradeNext chapterMansa Musa's hajj

In 1235, the Mandinka leader Sundiata Keita defeated King Sumanguru of Sosso at the Battle of Kirina and established the Mali Empire. Mali controlled the West African gold and salt trades, with its capital at Niani becoming a center of learning. The empire reached its zenith under Mansa Musa (1312-1337); during his pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324 he distributed so much gold in Cairo that the Egyptian gold price dropped for a decade. The Sankore University in Timbuktu became one of the world's most famous Islamic centers of learning.

Mali replaced Ghana and extended West African networks south to the upper Niger. An empire’s durability depended on keeping different routes and peoples within one order.
