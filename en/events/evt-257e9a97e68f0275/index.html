Tiglath-Pileser III overthrew Ashur-nirari V and ascended the throne. During his reign, he carried out comprehensive reforms of the empire's internal administration, military, and governance, establishing a provincial system and a professional army, ushering the Assyrian Empire into its peak period and beginning the glorious era of the Neo-Assyrian Empire.

Tiglath-Pileser III replaced temporary levies with a standing professional army and turned client states into provinces. Institutions mattered more than a single victory: they allowed the empire to campaign year after year.
