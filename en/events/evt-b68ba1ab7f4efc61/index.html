Chapter 8 of 8
Previous chapterFirst northern expedition, loss at Jieting

In 234, during his fifth northern expedition, Zhuge Liang faced Sima Yi at the Wu Zhang Plains, modern Qishan, Shaanxi, for more than a hundred days. The event record says he implemented military-agricultural colonies to prepare for a long war, but overwork led to illness and he died in camp.

On his deathbed, he ordered Yang Yi, Jiang Wei, and others to keep the death secret and withdraw to Shu. Zhuge Liang's death was a great loss to Shu Han, and his northern expedition plans were laid aside. Liu Shan posthumously honored him as Marquis Zhongwu; later generations respectfully call him Marquis Wu.

In the person-centered line, this chapter closes the story: from the Longzhong Plan to the Wu Zhang Plains, alliance, internal governance, and the rhythm of the expeditions run through it all. The record does not detail the handover of power in Shu after the withdrawal.
