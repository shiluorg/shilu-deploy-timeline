Sui builds the Renshou Palace

Event
c. 593 CESuiSocietyLegacy import · pending source reviewL1 · Structure checked · AI expansion

Volume position: Sui Dynasty · Ch. 2/7

Locate on the globe ↗Compare civilizations ↗More in this year ↗

Emperor Wen of the Sui Dynasty ordered Yang Su to build Renshou Palace in Qizhou (today's Fengxiang, Shaanxi Province) as a summer palace.1 Yang Su's supervision was strict and tens of thousands of servants died, but the palace was extremely magnificent, reflecting the strong national power in the early Sui Dynasty.2 The palace combined royal daily life with summer vacations, and the construction period and manpower were extremely compressed; grand projects were not only a display of national power, but also a consumption of people's power.3 The prosperity and harsh government of the early Sui Dynasty appeared at the same time.4
