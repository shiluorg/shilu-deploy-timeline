Napoleon

Person
Recorded range · 1769 CE — 1812 CE10 event anchors0 storiesAlso recorded as 拿破仑 / 拿破仑·波拿巴

Napoleon Bonaparte (1769–1821) was born in Corsica, entered French military school as a youth and rose during the French Revolution. He campaigned in Italy in 1796 and Egypt in 1798, seized power in the Brumaire coup of 1799 and became First Consul. In 1804 he crowned himself emperor and founded the First French Empire; he issued the Civil Code, reorganized finance, education and administration, and expanded the army through conscription and the corps system. From 1805 to 1809 he defeated coalitions at Austerlitz, Jena and elsewhere and controlled much of Europe; the Russian campaign of 1812 destroyed his army, he abdicated in 1814, returned for the Hundred Days in 1815, lost at Waterloo and was exiled to St. Helena, where he died in 1821. Napoleon turned revolutionary France into a bureaucratic military state; his conquests spread the code and nationalism but also brought occupation, plunder and war casualties. His Continental System hurt European economies and stimulated nationalist resistance.
