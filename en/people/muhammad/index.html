Muhammad

Person
Recorded range · 570 CE — 1481 CE10 event anchors0 storiesAlso recorded as 穆罕默德 / 先知穆罕默德

Muhammad (c. 570–632) was born in Mecca into the Hashim clan of Quraysh and worked as a merchant, known for honesty. Around 610 he began receiving revelations in the cave of Hira, proclaiming one God, the last judgment and social justice, and encountered opposition from Meccan elites. In 622 he migrated to Medina, where he built a Muslim community, established a charter and organized military and taxation; in 630 he entered Mecca peacefully, removed the idols and established the Kaaba as the religious center. He died in 632 without naming a clear successor, leaving the division between Sunni and Shia. His sayings and actions were transmitted as hadith, while the Quran is regarded as revelation. He was a religious prophet, lawgiver, military leader and community head, roles inseparable in Islamic tradition. The year of his migration became the first year of the Islamic calendar, and the Constitution of Medina is often read as an early text of plural community governance. In Medina he concluded agreements with Jewish and pagan tribes, forming a city community held together by religious covenant.
