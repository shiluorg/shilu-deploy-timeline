Leonardo da Vinci

Person
Recorded range · 1452 CE — 1519 CE6 event anchors0 storiesAlso recorded as 达·芬奇 / 达芬奇 / 列奥纳多·达·芬奇

Leonardo da Vinci (1452–1519) was born in Vinci, Italy, and entered Verrocchio's workshop in Florence as a youth, learning painting along with mechanics, metalwork and perspective. In the 1480s he moved to Milan, where he designed festivals, weapons and hydraulic works for the Sforza family and painted The Last Supper. He left extensive notebooks on anatomy, flight, water, optics and geometry; many designs remained on paper, yet they display a method that joined observation, drawing and engineering. He returned to Florence in the 1500s, painted the Mona Lisa and studied the human body and bird flight. In his last years he accepted an invitation from the French court and died at Amboise in 1519. Leonardo was not a modern scientist, and his knowledge was often entangled with court patronage and unfinished projects, but his notebooks became central to the Renaissance image of the universal genius. In his last years, with his right hand paralyzed, he still dictated anatomical notes; the Mona Lisa entered the French royal collection unfinished.
