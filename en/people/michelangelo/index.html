Michelangelo

Person
Recorded range · 1475 CE — 1597 CE8 event anchors0 storiesAlso recorded as 米开朗基罗 / 米开朗琪罗

Michelangelo (1475–1564) was born in Caprese and entered the Medici circle in Florence as a youth, studying sculpture and fresco. He completed the Pietà in 1498–1501 and carved David in 1501–1504, using anatomy and tension to express the civic ideal of the Republic. He painted the Sistine Chapel ceiling in 1508–1512 and completed the Last Judgment in 1536–1541. In later life he directed the design of St. Peter's Basilica, work that continued after his death. Michelangelo brought a sculptor's sense of mass into painting and combined Neoplatonic ideas with religious feeling; his works became benchmarks of the High Renaissance, while his temperament, patronage relationships and unfinished projects also shaped his image. He died in Rome in 1564. He never married and treated artistic creation as a religious vocation, an image later amplified by Romanticism. His surviving poetry and late architectural drawings were also widely studied by later artists and historians.
