Genghis Khan

Person
Recorded range · 1162 CE — 1227 CE10 event anchors0 storiesAlso recorded as 成吉思汗 / 铁木真

Genghis Khan (c. 1162–1227), born Temujin on the Onon River, lost his father as a boy and saw his family reduced to poverty and factional conflict. After years of alliances and warfare he was proclaimed Genghis Khan in 1206 and unified the Mongolian plateau. He then campaigned against the Jin, Western Xia and the Khwarazmian empire in Central Asia, expanding through cavalry mobility, intelligence, terror and a system of appanages. He died in 1227 during the campaign against Western Xia. Genghis Khan did not build a stable civil bureaucracy, but he transformed steppe tribes into a trans-regional military aristocracy; his successors founded the Yuan, Ilkhanate, Chagatai and Golden Horde, creating networks of trade and movement across Eurasia as well as large-scale slaughter and demographic loss. His armies linked post stations, trade routes and the movement of craftsmen, and the Black Death may also have spread along these networks. He issued the Yasa and distributed appanages to his sons, turning loyalty and military achievement into new political capital, but the succession problem was never solved.
