Caracalla and the Constitutio Antoniniana (212 CE)

SourceSources
Caracalla and the Constitutio Antoniniana (212 CE) · Encyclopaedia Britannica (accessed 2026-11-25) Original source ↗

Only the grant of citizenship to all free inhabitants, the possible tax motive, and the earlier gradual extension are used; the motive is not treated as a single settled conclusion.

选段

His principal achievements were his colossal baths in Rome and his edict of 212, giving Roman citizenship to all free inhabitants of the empire.

The Edict of Caracalla, issued in 212 CE by Roman Emperor Caracalla, granted Roman citizenship to all free inhabitants of the Roman Empire.

Caracalla's reign, marked by violence and megalomania, aimed to increase revenue by expanding tax obligations to a broader population. While citizenship had been gradually extended, the Edict made it universal, diminishing its significance as a distinction and altering commemorative practices.

...ancient sources... slander Caracalla’s most famous measure, the so-called Constitutio Antoniniana de Civitate, as a device designed solely to collect more taxes.

使用边界

  • 写“212 年授予帝国所有自由居民罗马公民权”，不写“所有自由男性/女性”这种条目未细分的表述。
  • 增税动机写“可能/古代史料指责”，不写成已证实的唯一原因。
  • 公民权此前已逐步扩展，212 年使其普遍化；不写“此前只有罗马城居民有公民权”。
  • 不写“公民权政治价值立刻归零”；只写“作为身份区分的意义下降”。
