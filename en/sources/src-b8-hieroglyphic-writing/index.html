Hieroglyphic writing

SourceSources
Hieroglyphic writing · Encyclopaedia Britannica (accessed 2026-11-25) Original source ↗

Only the date of the earliest inscriptions, phonetic character, roughly 700 signs, range of uses and limited literate circle are used; the entry is not made to say hieroglyphs were fully mature by 3200 BCE or were mere pictures.

选段

The most ancient hieroglyphs date from the end of the 4th millennium bce and comprise annotations incised onto pottery jars and ivory plaques deposited in tombs, presumably for the purpose of identification of the dead.

This circumstance means that hieroglyphs were from the very beginning phonetic symbols. An earlier stage consisting exclusively of picture writing using actual illustrations of the intended words cannot be shown to have existed in Egypt; indeed, such a stage can with great probability be ruled out.

Hieroglyphic writing was used as much for secular texts—historical inscriptions, songs, legal documents, scientific documents—as for religious subject matter—cult rituals, myths, hymns, grave inscriptions of all kinds, and prayers.

At all periods only a limited circle understood the hieroglyphic script. Only those who needed the knowledge in their professions acquired the arts of writing and reading.

For popular use, hieratic script was developed, an abbreviated form of the picture symbols such as would naturally develop in writing with brush and ink on smooth surfaces such as papyrus, wood, and limestone.

使用边界

  • 写“最早象形文字在前4千纪末，刻在陶罐和象牙牌上”，不写“前3200年已有成熟文本”。
  • 写“从一开始就使用表音符号，不存在纯图画阶段”，不写“象形文字先是图画、后来变成字母”。
  • 写“约700个符号稳定使用两千多年”，不写“符号数量一直很少变化是因为没人创新”。
  • 写“只有官员、祭司、工匠等少数人掌握；日常书写用僧侣体”，不写“全民识字”。
