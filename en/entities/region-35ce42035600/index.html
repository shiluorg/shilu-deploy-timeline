421 CE

Liu Yu kills Emperor Gong of Jin, ends any possibility of Jin restoration

421 CE

Liu Song expeditionary force eliminates Northern Liang remnants in Hexi, recovers some territory

422 CE

Large-scale conflict between Liu Song and Northern Wei along the Yellow River

422 CE

Emperor Wu of Song dies; Emperor Shao of Song ascends

423 CE

Xu Xianzhi etc. depose Emperor Shao of Song, install Emperor Wen of Song

424 CE

Emperor Wen of Song executes Xu Xianzhi and Fu Liang, eliminates the regents

425 CE

Emperor Wen of Song promotes Confucianism, establishes four academies

426 CE

Tao Yuanming dies of illness

428 CE

Yuanjia administration reaches its peak; political stability achieved

429 CE

Border conflict between Liu Song and Northern Wei in the Huai River region

430 CE

Liu Song northern expedition fails; all territory south of the Yellow River lost

432 CE

Zhao Guang rebellion breaks out in Liu Song's Yizhou, lasting six months

433 CE

Liu Song poet Xie Lingyun executed by Emperor Wen of Song

434 CE

Continued conflict between Liu Song and Northern Wei in the Ru and Ying river valleys

437 CE

Emperor Wen of Song establishes Four Academies, greatly develops scholarship

442 CE

Liu Song Prince Liu Jun stationed at Jingkou, strengthens northern defenses

444 CE

Liu Song court discusses another northern expedition but abandons it due to internal opposition

446 CE

Liu Song and Northern Wei clash in the Runan and Xincai region

449 CE

Liu Song launches massive northern expedition — the second Yuanjia northern expedition

450 CE

Liu Song's Yuanjia northern expedition ends in disaster; Jiangbei region savagely ravaged

452 CE

Liu Song crown prince Liu Shao conflicts with Emperor Wen; political turmoil

453 CE

Liu Song civil war: Liu Jun raises troops to attack Liu Shao

454 CE

Liu Jun captures Jiankang, executes Liu Shao, ascends throne

455 CE

Emperor Xiaowu of Song suppresses rebellion of Prince of Jingling, Liu Dan

456 CE

Intra-imperial clan strife in Liu Song; several princes killed

457 CE

Emperor Xiaowu of Song implements new policies to weaken aristocratic clans

458 CE

Liu Song general Shen Qingzhi suppresses peasant rebellion in eastern Zhejiang

459 CE

Shen Qingzhi again suppresses rebellion of Prince of Jingling, Liu Dan

461 CE

Mutual slaughter among Liu Song imperial princes

462 CE

Zu Chongzhi of Liu Song compiles the Daming Calendar, accurately calculates tropical year

463 CE

Emperor Xiaowu of Song dies; Emperor Qianfei of Song (Liu Ziye) ascends

464 CE

Emperor Qianfei of Song kills many imperial clansmen and ministers, alienating everyone

465 CE

Liu Yu kills Liu Ziye, proclaims himself emperor

466 CE

Yijia Calamity in Liu Song: imperial princes unite against Emperor Ming

467 CE

Emperor Ming of Song suppresses Yijia Calamity, massacres many imperial clansmen

470 CE

Great famine in Nanxuzhou of Liu Song; people suffer terribly

471 CE

Emperor Ming of Song dies; Emperor Houfei of Song (Liu Yu) ascends

472 CE

Liu Song minister Xiao Daocheng kills Emperor Houfei of Song, establishes Emperor Shun of Song (Liu Zhun)

473 CE

Liu Song prince Liu Xiufan rebels; Xiao Daocheng suppresses

474 CE

Xiao Daocheng appointed Grand Commandant, takes full control of Liu Song military and civil power

475 CE

Liu Song Emperor Houfei becomes tyrannical; court and populace alienated

476 CE

Xiao Daocheng kills Liu Yu, seizes control of Jiankang

477 CE

Liu Song Inspector of Jingzhou Shen Youzhi raises troops against Xiao Daocheng

478 CE

Xiao Daocheng defeats Shen Youzhi, takes full control of Liu Song
