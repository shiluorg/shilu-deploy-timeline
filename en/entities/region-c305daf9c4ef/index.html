OPEN HISTORY

Shu Han

NameNames
An imported name and related records, not a verified political lifespan or modern territorial claim.
219 CE

Guan Yu launches the Fancheng campaign, floods seven armies, terrifies the Central Plains

219 CE

Liu Bei proclaims himself King of Hanzhong at Mianyang and establishes the Hanzhong King's office

221 CE

Liu Bei ascends the imperial throne at Chengdu; his dynasty is called Han, known to history as Shu Han

221 CE

To avenge Guan Yu, Liu Bei mobilizes 70,000 troops to attack Wu

222 CE

Liu Shan succeeds in Chengdu; Zhuge Liang opens his office and restores the alliance with Wu

223 CE

Zhuge Liang sends Deng Zhi as envoy to Wu to repair bilateral relations

224 CE

Zhuge Liang vigorously develops production and prepares the military in Chengdu

225 CE

Zhuge Liang's southern campaign: captures Meng Huo seven times, pacifies the Nanzhong rebellion

227 CE

Zhuge Liang presents the 'Chu Shi Biao', leads his army to Hanzhong to prepare for the northern expedition

228 CE

Zhuge Liang's second northern expedition: besieges Chencang but fails to take it

228 CE

Zhuge Liang's first northern expedition: Ma Su loses Jieting, leading to complete failure

229 CE

Zhuge Liang's third northern expedition: takes Wudu and Yinping commanderies

229 CE

Shu Han and Eastern Wu conclude a formal alliance, agreeing to jointly attack Wei

231 CE

Zhuge Liang's fourth northern expedition: uses wooden oxen for transport, confronts Sima Yi

232 CE

Zhuge Liang rests and refurbishes in Hanzhong, improving weapons and making repeating crossbows and wooden oxen/flowing horses

233 CE

Zhuge Liang implements military‑agricultural colonies south of the Wei River, storing grain for the fifth northern expedition

234 CE

Zhuge Liang's fifth northern expedition: exits the Xie Valley, encamps at Wu Zhang Plains

234 CE

Zhuge Liang dies of illness at the Wu Zhang Plains camp, aged 54

234 CE

After the Shu army withdraws, Wei Yan and Yang Yi turn on each other; Wei Yan is killed

235 CE

Jiang Wan takes charge of Shu Han affairs after Zhuge Liang, continuing the plans for northern expeditions

242 CE

Shu Han's Grand General Jiang Wan plans a river‑borne invasion of Wei from the Han and Mian rivers, but does not implement it

243 CE

Fei Yi becomes Grand General of Shu Han, continuing Jiang Wan's policy of rest and recuperation

247 CE

Rebellion of minority tribes breaks out on Shu Han's Liangzhou border; Jiang Wei leads army to pacify it

253 CE

Shu Han Grand General Fei Yi is assassinated by a surrendered Wei general

255 CE

Shu Han's Jiang Wei conducts a northern expedition, defeating Wei general Wang Jing at Didao

256 CE

After his victory at Taoxi, Jiang Wei besieges Didao but is repelled

261 CE

Shu Han's Jiang Wei, to avoid the eunuch Huang Hao, goes to Tazhong to farm and avoid trouble

262 CE

Jiang Wei's last northern expedition is defeated by Deng Ai; he never again undertakes a campaign

263 CE

Jiang Wei pretends to surrender to Zhong Hui, plots to restore Shu, but the plot fails and he is killed
