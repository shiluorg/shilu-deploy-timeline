OPEN HISTORY

Northern Wei

NameNames
An imported name and related records, not a verified political lifespan or modern territorial claim.
385 CE

Tuoba Gui re-establishes the Dai state at Niuchuan, the prototype of Northern Wei

386 CE

Tuoba Gui changes his title to King of Wei, establishing the Northern Wei state name

389 CE

Northern Wei's Tuoba Gui defeats the Xiongnu Tiefu tribe, unifying the Daibei region

391 CE

Northern Wei defeats the remnants of the Tiefu Xiongnu, completely controlling the Hetao region

395 CE

Later Yan suffers a devastating defeat at the Battle of Canhe Slope by Northern Wei, losing its elite forces

396 CE

Tuoba Gui of Northern Wei formally proclaims himself emperor, establishing the Northern Wei dynasty

398 CE

Northern Wei formally moves its capital to Pingcheng, building palaces

405 CE

Emperor Daowu of Northern Wei implements policy of scattering tribes

422 CE

Emperor Mingyuan of Northern Wei dies; Emperor Taiwu ascends

423 CE

Northern Wei captures Hulao Pass; Liu Song northern defenses collapse

424 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei personally campaigns against Rouran, defeats Chile

426 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei captures Chang'an, Xia forces withdraw from Guanzhong

427 CE

Northern Wei captures Tongwan City, destroys main Xia army

429 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei launches second major campaign against Rouran, carves inscription

435 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei leads campaign against Northern Yan, besieges Helong

436 CE

Northern Wei captures Helong, destroys Northern Yan

439 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei leads western expedition, destroys Northern Liang, unifies north

440 CE

Northern Liang remnants launch revolt in Hexi Corridor

441 CE

Northern Wei dispatches troops to destroy Qiuci state, controlling Longnan

443 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei again campaigns north against Rouran; Rouran khagan Tughuzhen flees north

445 CE

Lu Shui Hu chief Gai Wu revolts at Xingcheng, contacts Liu Song

446 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei issues edict to destroy Buddhism, burn scriptures and images

448 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei launches sixth campaign against Rouran, conquers northern Tianshan

451 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei leads southern campaign, reaches Guabu, threatens Jiankang

452 CE

Emperor Taiwu of Northern Wei murdered by eunuch Zong Ai; Emperor Wencheng ascends

458 CE

Emperor Wencheng of Northern Wei restores Buddhism; excavation of Yungang Grottoes begins

460 CE

Large-scale excavation of Yungang Grottoes makes it a center of Buddhist art in northern China

466 CE

Northern Wei takes advantage of Liu Song's civil war to occupy Qingzhou and Xuzhou

468 CE

Emperor Wencheng of Northern Wei dies; Emperor Xianwen (Tuoba Hong) ascends

469 CE

Liu Song loses all four Huabei provinces, retreats to Huai River

471 CE

Emperor Xianwen of Northern Wei abdicates; Emperor Xiaowen Yuan Hong ascends

484 CE

Empress Dowager Feng of Northern Wei issues salary system, reorganizes officialdom

485 CE

Northern Wei implements equal-field system and three-elders system, deepening Sinicization reforms

490 CE

Empress Dowager Feng of Northern Wei dies; Emperor Xiaowen begins personal rule

491 CE

The Hanging Temple is built at Hengshan

491 CE

Emperor Xiaowen of Northern Wei reforms official system, promotes Sinicization

494 CE

Emperor Xiaowen of Northern Wei announces completion of capital relocation to Luoyang, fully implements Sinicization

495 CE

Large-scale excavation of Longmen Grottoes begins

495 CE

Emperor Xiaowen orders Xianbei surnames changed to Han surnames, inscribed on steles

496 CE

Northern Wei crown prince Yuan Xun opposes Sinicization, is deposed and killed

497 CE

Emperor Xiaowen of Northern Wei launches southern campaign, captures Nanyang and Xinye

498 CE

Emperor Xiaowen of Northern Wei again campaigns south, attacks Yiyang

499 CE

Emperor Xiaowen of Northern Wei dies; Emperor Xuanwu (Yuan Ke) ascends

509 CE

Northern Wei expands Luoyang on a large scale

513 CE

Empress Dowager Hu begins to interfere in Northern Wei politics

515 CE

Emperor Xuanwu of Northern Wei dies; Empress Dowager Hu formally assumes regency

519 CE

Erzhu Rong rises up and enters Luoyang

520 CE

Erzhu Rong installs Emperor Xiaozhuang and takes control of Northern Wei government

522 CE

Emperor Xiaozhuang of Northern Wei kills Erzhu Rong

523 CE

The Songyue Pagoda is completed

523 CE

Outbreak of the Six Frontier Town rebellions in Northern Wei

524 CE

Six Frontier Town rebellions expand; Northern Wei crisis deepens

525 CE

Northern Wei uses Rouran troops to suppress rebellions

526 CE

Ge Rong leads the Hebei rebel army

528 CE

Empress Dowager Hu of Northern Wei poisons Emperor Xiaoming

528 CE

Erzhu Rong defeats Ge Rong and pacifies the Hebei rebellion

531 CE

Gao Huan rebels against the Erzhu clan

532 CE

Gao Huan captures Luoyang and destroys the Erzhu clan

534 CE

Northern Wei splits into Eastern Wei and Western Wei

540 CE

Jia Sixie authors "Qimin Yaoshu" (Essential Techniques for the Welfare of the People)
