Eastern Wu

NameNames
An imported name and related records, not a verified political lifespan or modern territorial claim.
210 CE

Eastern Wu general Zhou Yu dies of illness at Baqiu, aged 36

212 CE

Sun Quan moves his capital to Moling and builds Stone City, renaming it Jianye

215 CE

Sun Quan sends Zhuge Jin to negotiate with Liu Bei; dispute over Jingzhou temporarily eased

219 CE

Lü Meng captures Jiangling in a 'white‑sailed boat' ruse; Guan Yu flees to Maicheng and is killed

221 CE

Emperor Wen of Wei (Cao Pi) enfeoffs Sun Quan as King of Wu with the Nine Bestowals and appoints him Governor of Jingzhou

222 CE

Sun Quan accepts Wei titles but refuses to send a hostage; proclaims himself King of Wu and adopts reign name Huangwu

228 CE

Eastern Wu's Lu Xun destroys Cao Xiu at Shiting, shocking the entire Wei state

229 CE

Sun Quan formally proclaims himself emperor at Wuchang, with the dynasty name Wu, era name Huanglong

229 CE

After proclaiming himself emperor, Sun Quan formally moves the capital to Jianye

230 CE

Sun Quan sends generals Wei Wen and Zhuge Zhi with a fleet of 10,000 men to seek the legendary island of Yizhou (Taiwan)

241 CE

Eastern Wu's Quan Cong attacks Wei, is defeated

245 CE

Eastern Wu digs the Pogandu Canal, linking the Qinhuai River and the Lake Tai basin

250 CE

Sun Quan, due to a succession dispute, dethrones his eldest son Sun He, causing internal turmoil in Eastern Wu

252 CE

Sun Quan, Emperor Da of Wu, dies at the age of 71

c. 252 CE

Zhuge Ke, as Grand Tutor, takes charge of Eastern Wu's government, becoming a powerful regent

253 CE

Eastern Wu regent Zhuge Ke leads 200,000 troops north and suffers a disastrous defeat at Xincheng

254 CE

Eastern Wu regent Sun Jun kills Zhuge Ke and takes control

256 CE

Eastern Wu regent Sun Jun dies; his cousin Sun Chen seizes power

258 CE

Eastern Wu's Sun Chen deposes Sun Liang and installs Sun Xiu; Sun Xiu then kills Sun Chen

264 CE

Sun Hao of Eastern Wu ascends the throne, known as the Last Emperor of Wu

268 CE

Sun Hao, Last Emperor of Wu, becomes more tyrannical, killing loyal ministers, making Eastern Wu politics even darker

272 CE

Sun Hao, the tyrant of Eastern Wu, kills members of Lu Kang's family; Lu Kang, though angry, dares not speak out
