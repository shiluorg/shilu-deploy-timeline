奥斯曼国家的起源（约1300年）

选段

The Ottoman Empire began at the very end of the 13th century with a series of raids from Turkic warriors (known as ghazis) led by Osman I, a prince (bey) whose father, Ertugrul, had established a power base in Söğüt (near Bursa, Turkey).

Following the final Mongol defeat of the Seljuqs in 1293, Osman emerged as prince (bey) of the border principality that took over Byzantine Bithynia in northwestern Anatolia.

By 1300 Osman ruled an area in Anatolia stretching from Eskişehir (Dorylaeum) to the plains of İznik (Nicaea), having defeated several organized Byzantine efforts to curb his expansion.

The term Ottoman is a dynastic appellation derived from Osman I (Arabic: ʿUthmān), the nomadic Turkmen chief who founded both the dynasty and the empire about 1300.

Osman's grandson Murad I laid the foundation for an institutionalized Ottoman state, continued by Murad's son Bayezid I.

Bayezid I, "the Thunderbolt," victor at the Battle of Nicopolis in 1396, was defeated by the Turkic ruler Timur at the Battle of Ankara on July 20, 1402. Bayezid's humiliating loss came close to destroying the Ottoman Empire.

使用边界

  • 1299 是后世王朝叙事常用的起点，本选段只支持“13 世纪末”“约 1300 年”，不把建国写成有明确仪式的单日事件。
  • 不写“奥斯曼在卡拉贾希萨尔宣布独立、不再承认罗姆苏丹国宗主权”；本选段没有这些细节。
  • 不把早期扩张写成单纯宗教战争；原文强调加齐袭掠、塞尔柱瓦解、蒙古宗主权和拜占庭边防松动。
  • 1402 年安卡拉之战只用于说明尼科波利斯的胜利没有带来永久霸权，不展开帖木儿战争全过程。
