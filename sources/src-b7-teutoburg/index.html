条顿堡森林战役（公元9年）

来源来源
条顿堡森林战役（公元9年） · Encyclopaedia Britannica (访问于 2026-11-25) 原始来源 ↗

仅使用条目明确支持的核心事实句；不把“罗马永久放弃日耳曼”写成绝对结论。

选段

The Battle of the Teutoburg Forest occurred in the autumn of 9 CE when Arminius, a German tribal leader, organized ambushes on three Roman legions led by Publius Quinctilius Varus. Over four days, Arminius and his insurgents destroyed all three legions, which stopped Rome from conquering Germania east of the Rhine River.

In the meantime, Publius Quinctilius Varus acted as governor of the proto-province of Germania. He had command of the three legions constituting the army of Germania Inferior: Legio XVII, Legio XVIII, and Legio XIX.

In total, nearly 20,000 Romans were killed in the engagement, while German losses were minimal.

Tiberius waged three subsequent campaigns against the Germanic tribes, but he chose not to occupy their land east of the Rhine. Instead, he stationed at least eight legions along the frontier to safeguard the provinces of eastern Gaul against a Germanic invasion.

The historian Suetonius writes that Augustus went months without cutting his beard or hair—a sign of mourning in Roman religious tradition—and often wailed, “Quinctilius Varus, give me back my legions!”

使用边界

  • 日期写“公元9年秋”，不写具体日。
  • 三个军团写 XVII、XVIII、XIX；不写“三个军团加辅助部队共 2 万”这种未见于条目的精确拆分。
  • 伤亡写“近 20,000 罗马人死亡，日耳曼方损失很小”；不写“全军覆没、一个不剩”。
  • 后果写“罗马停止征服莱茵河以东、改以驻军守边”，不写“永久放弃日耳曼地区”这种绝对句。
  • 奥古斯都的悲痛引苏埃托尼乌斯，标明是后世传记记载，不当作现场记录。
